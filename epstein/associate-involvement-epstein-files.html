⚖️ Legal & Ethical Notes

Presumption of Innocence: All individuals referenced in this entry who have not been convicted of crimes are presumed innocent under U.S. law. Allegations presented here are from civil pleadings and have not been proven in criminal court. This entry does not assert the truth of unadjudicated claims.

Victim Protection: No minor, victim, or survivor is identified in this entry. Language is trauma-informed. All references to victims use generic terms or refer only to publicly named parties in court documents where identity was already part of the public record through voluntary testimony.

Civil vs. Criminal Distinction: This entry clearly distinguishes between allegations in civil litigation (which use a "preponderance of evidence" standard) and criminal convictions (which require proof "beyond reasonable doubt"). Outcomes in civil cases (settlements, dismissals) are not presented as judicial findings of factual guilt.

Source Transparency: Every claim about what documents contain is attributed to a specific, verifiable court filing. Speculation about the truthfulness of allegations is explicitly avoided. Claims are labeled as "alleged" or "disputed" where appropriate.

Ongoing Review: This entry will be updated within 14 days if: (1) a court issues a ruling that adjudicates any of the allegations cited; (2) new documents are unsealed that materially change the context of these claims; or (3) criminal charges are filed that relate to these allegations. Corrections will be prominently noted with date and nature of change.
