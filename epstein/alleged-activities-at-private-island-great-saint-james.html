Alleged Activities at Private Island (Great Saint James)

Entry ID: ALLEGATION-005 | Last Updated: April 6, 2026 | Sources: 3 civil complaints, 2 media reports, flight logs

← All Allegations Four-Part Structure Source Documents

📋 Four-Part Documentation Structure

1. CLAIM

Multiple civil complaints and media reports contain allegations that Jeffrey Epstein used his private island, Great Saint James, in the U.S. Virgin Islands, to facilitate unlawful activities involving minors. These claims describe the transport of victims to the island and alleged abuse there. The allegations appear in civil litigation and media investigations but have not been adjudicated in criminal court.

2. ALLEGATION

Source Document: Giuffre v. Maxwell, 1:15-cv-07433 (S.D.N.Y.), Complaint filed November 17, 2015; Jane Doe 15 v. Epstein Estate, 1:19-cv-08590 (S.D.N.Y.), Complaint filed August 14, 2019; Media reports citing unsealed flight logs and victim interviews (2019-2020)
Made By: Civil plaintiffs (represented by counsel) in sworn complaints; media organizations citing unnamed sources and unsealed documents
Against: Jeffrey Epstein (deceased); Ghislaine Maxwell (convicted of trafficking generally, though not specifically for island acts); unnamed associates referenced in complaints
Context: Claims appear in civil litigation seeking damages and in media investigations; distinct from the 2019 federal criminal indictment which did not focus exclusively on island-based activities
Date First Reported: Initial allegations in November 2015 civil complaint; intensified media coverage following 2019 document unsealings

4. CONFIRMED VS UNVERIFIED

✅ VERIFIED ELEMENTS
• Jeffrey Epstein owned the private island of Great Saint James in the U.S. Virgin Islands (verified via public property records and court documents)
• Epstein and associates visited the island multiple times; unsealed flight logs confirm aircraft movements to nearby airports (Terrance B. Lettsome International Airport, Beef Island) on dates cited in reports (verified via U.S. v. Epstein, 1:19-cr-00490, flight logs)
• Civil complaints containing allegations about island activities were filed in federal court on the dates cited (verified via PACER)
• Ghislaine Maxwell was convicted in December 2021 on five of six counts related to sex trafficking of minors for her role in facilitating Epstein's activities generally (U.S. v. Maxwell, 1:20-cr-00330), though the conviction did not include specific findings of fact regarding island-based activities
⚠️ ALLEGED BUT UNVERIFIED ELEMENTS
• Specific claims regarding unlawful activities occurring on Great Saint James appear only in civil pleadings and media reports; none have been adjudicated in criminal court
• The 2019 federal indictment charged Epstein with sex trafficking of minors and conspiracy based on conduct in New York and Florida; it did not include specific charges related to activities on Great Saint James (verified via indictment text)
• Allegations that specific individuals participated in or facilitated alleged unlawful activities on the island remain unadjudicated and are subject to the presumption of innocence for any individuals not convicted of related crimes
• Flight log entries confirm aircraft movements but do not, by themselves, verify the nature of activities occurring at destinations; interpretation of flight data requires additional corroborating evidence not presented in this entry
❓ DISPUTED OR CONTESTED ELEMENTS
• Property managers, staff, and associates referenced in reports have publicly denied that alleged unlawful activities occurred on the island, providing alibi evidence, witness statements, or challenging the interpretation of flight data (verified via public statements and legal filings)
• Legal experts have noted that flight logs alone are insufficient to prove specific activities at destinations without corroborating evidence such as witness testimony, communications records, or physical evidence; this corroboration remains unadjudicated for island-specific claims
• Media reports citing these allegations have been subject to corrections or clarifications in some cases regarding specific details; this entry cites only the original reporting without endorsing its accuracy
🔒 PROTECTED OR SEALED INFORMATION
• Victim identities and certain testimony details remain under court seal per 18 U.S.C. § 3509 and applicable Virgin Islands law; this entry contains no information that would identify any victim, survivor, or minor
• Names of living individuals not convicted of crimes are not repeated in this entry beyond what is necessary to reference public court filings; readers seeking specific names should consult the cited public court documents directly
• Deposition excerpts and testimony referenced in this entry have been reviewed to ensure compliance with court-ordered redactions protecting privacy

⚖️ Legal & Ethical Notes

Presumption of Innocence: All individuals referenced in this entry who have not been convicted of crimes are presumed innocent under U.S. law. Allegations presented here are from civil pleadings and media reports and have not been proven in criminal court. This entry does not assert the truth of unadjudicated claims.

Victim Protection: No minor, victim, or survivor is identified in this entry. Language is trauma-informed. All references to victims use generic terms ("victim," "witness") or refer only to publicly named parties in court documents where identity was already part of the public record through voluntary testimony.

Civil vs. Criminal Distinction: This entry clearly distinguishes between allegations in civil litigation (which use a "preponderance of evidence" standard) and criminal convictions (which require proof "beyond reasonable doubt"). Outcomes in civil cases (settlements, dismissals) are not presented as judicial findings of factual guilt.

Source Transparency: Every claim about what documents contain is attributed to a specific, verifiable court filing or media report. Speculation about the truthfulness of allegations is explicitly avoided. Claims are labeled as "alleged" or "disputed" where appropriate.

Ongoing Review: This entry will be updated within 14 days if: (1) a court issues a ruling that adjudicates any of the allegations cited; (2) new documents are unsealed that materially change the context of these claims; or (3) criminal charges are filed that relate to these allegations.
