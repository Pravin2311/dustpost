Trafficking of Minors for Sexual Acts: Adjudicated Facts

📋 Four-Part Documentation Structure

1. CLAIM

Jeffrey Epstein engaged in the sex trafficking of minors, recruiting, transporting, and providing individuals under the age of 18 for commercial sex acts between approximately 2002 and 2005, with conduct continuing in some form through 2018.

2. ALLEGATION

Source Document: United States v. Jeffrey Epstein, 1:19-cr-00490 (S.D.N.Y.), Indictment filed July 8, 2019; Plea Agreement, United States v. Epstein, 08-80736-CR (S.D. Fla.), 2008
Made By: Federal prosecutors (Southern District of New York); Multiple victim-witnesses in sworn testimony
Against: Jeffrey Epstein (deceased); Ghislaine Maxwell (convicted December 2021)
Context: Core charges in federal criminal prosecution; basis for 2008 non-prosecution agreement and 2019 indictment
Date First Reported: Initial investigation public in 2006; detailed allegations in 2019 indictment unsealed July 2019

3. REPORTED BY

Primary Source(s):

  • U.S. v. Epstein, 1:19-cr-00490 (S.D.N.Y.), Indictment, July 8, 2019 (public record via PACER)

  • Victim testimony excerpts, unsealed pursuant to court order in Giuffre v. Maxwell, 1:15-cv-07433 (S.D.N.Y.), 2019-2021

4. CONFIRMED VS UNVERIFIED

✅ VERIFIED ELEMENTS
• Epstein was charged with and pleaded guilty to state charges related to soliciting prostitution from a minor in Florida in 2008 (Plea Agreement, 08-80736-CR)
• The 2019 federal indictment charged Epstein with sex trafficking of minors and conspiracy, with a grand jury finding probable cause based on victim testimony, financial records, flight logs, and witness statements (U.S. v. Epstein, 1:19-cr-00490, Indictment ¶¶ 1-15)
• Ghislaine Maxwell was convicted in December 2021 on five of six counts related to sex trafficking of minors for her role in facilitating Epstein's activities (U.S. v. Maxwell, 1:20-cr-00330, Verdict Dec. 29, 2021)
• Multiple victims provided consistent, corroborated testimony about recruitment patterns, transportation methods, and abuse timelines, with corroboration from financial records, travel documents, and third-party witness statements (Unsealed testimony excerpts, Giuffre v. Maxwell, 2019-2021)
⚠️ ALLEGED BUT UNVERIFIED ELEMENTS
• Specific details about activities at non-U.S. locations mentioned in victim testimony remain under investigation by foreign authorities and have not been adjudicated in U.S. courts
• Allegations regarding the full scope of Epstein's network beyond convicted individuals appear in civil filings but have not been proven in criminal proceedings and remain subject to ongoing investigation
❓ DISPUTED OR CONTESTED ELEMENTS
• The scope and terms of the 2008 Non-Prosecution Agreement were contested by victims and later found by a federal judge to have violated the Crime Victims' Rights Act (Jane Doe 1 & 2 v. United States, 1:08-cv-80736, Order Feb. 21, 2019), but certain procedural aspects remain subject to ongoing legal review
• Specific allegations against individuals not charged or convicted are actively disputed by those individuals and have not been proven in court; this entry does not present such allegations as fact
🔒 PROTECTED OR SEALED INFORMATION
• Victim identities, certain testimony excerpts, and details involving minors remain under court seal per 18 U.S.C. § 3509 and applicable state victim protection laws
• This entry contains no information that would identify any victim, survivor, or minor; all references use generic terms ("victim," "witness") or refer only to publicly named parties in court documents
