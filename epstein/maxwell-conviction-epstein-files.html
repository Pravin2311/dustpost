📋 Four-Part Documentation Structure

1. CLAIM

Ghislaine Maxwell was convicted by a federal jury in December 2021 on five of six counts related to sex trafficking of minors and conspiracy for her role in facilitating Jeffrey Epstein's abuse. She was sentenced to 20 years in federal prison. Appeals regarding jury instructions and sufficiency of evidence are pending or have been resolved per appellate court dockets.
