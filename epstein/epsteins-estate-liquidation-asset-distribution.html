4. CONFIRMED VS UNVERIFIED

✅ VERIFIED ELEMENTS
• Probate proceedings were formally opened and administered under USVI statutory framework
• Court-appointed fiduciaries published asset inventories, liquidation timelines, and creditor claim processing procedures
• Judicial orders approved compensation distribution frameworks prioritizing verified victim claims per established legal standards
• Real estate, financial accounts, and corporate holdings were liquidated or transferred under court supervision with published accounting
⚠️ ALLEGED BUT UNVERIFIED ELEMENTS
• Claims that significant assets were concealed from probate inventory, transferred to undisclosed entities, or distributed outside court-approved frameworks lack substantiation in published fiduciary reports or judicial findings
• Allegations of preferential treatment for specific creditors or beneficiaries remain unproven absent court-ordered audit findings
❓ DISPUTED OR CONTESTED ELEMENTS
• Legal practitioners debate whether probate asset valuation methodologies adequately reflected market conditions at liquidation dates
• Creditor committees and victim advocates have held differing perspectives on distribution prioritization, though all recognized distributions followed court-approved formulas
🔒 PROTECTED OR SEALED INFORMATION
