• Multiple institutions published revised donor vetting protocols and gift acceptance policies following public review
• Federal agencies updated CVRA compliance guidance, enhancing victim notification and participation procedures
• Legislative proposals regarding institutional transparency and victim-centered oversight were introduced and debated
• Advocacy organizations published survivor-informed guidelines adopted by select institutions
⚠️ ALLEGATED BUT UNVERIFIED ELEMENTS
• Claims that policy reforms represent "systemic accountability" or "institutional reckoning" lack independent verification and conflate administrative procedure with cultural transformation

❓ DISPUTED OR CONTESTED ELEMENTS

🔒 PROTECTED OR SEALED INFORMATION
