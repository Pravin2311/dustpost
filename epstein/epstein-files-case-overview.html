Complete Timeline of Events

2005-2008: Initial Investigation & State Prosecution

  • 2005: Palm Beach Police Department opens investigation following complaints
  • 2006: FBI field office begins parallel federal review
  • 2007: Non-Prosecution Agreement (NPA) negotiated with U.S. Attorney's Office
  • 2008: Epstein pleads guilty to state charges; registers as sex offender

2015-2019: Civil Litigation & Renewed Scrutiny

  • 2015: Giuffre v. Maxwell civil complaint filed in S.D.N.Y.
  • 2018: Miami Herald "Perversion of Justice" investigative series published
  • 2019 (July): Epstein arrested on federal sex trafficking charges
  • 2019 (August): Epstein dies in federal custody at MCC New York

2019-2022: Estate Administration & Criminal Prosecution

  • 2019-2020: USVI probate proceedings; estate asset inventory published
  • 2020: Maxwell arrested; federal indictment unsealed
  • 2021: Maxwell trial and conviction on five counts
  • 2022: Maxwell sentenced to 20 years in federal prison

2020-2026: Ongoing Reviews & Institutional Reforms

  • 2020: DOJ OIG report on 2007-2008 federal investigation published
  • 2020-2022: Epstein Victims' Compensation Program operates
  • 2019-2023: Multiple institutional reviews and policy reforms published
  • 2023-2026: Continued document unsealing and administrative reviews

Victim Compensation Program

The Epstein Victims' Compensation Program (EVCP) was established in 2019 as a voluntary, independent compensation mechanism for individuals alleging abuse by Jeffrey Epstein.

Program Details

  • Operational Period: 2020-2022
  • Administration: Independent administrator with oversight committee
  • Funding Source: Epstein Estate assets
  • Eligibility: Individuals alleging sexual abuse by Epstein
  • Confidentiality: Individual claims and payouts remain sealed per court order

The program operated under court supervision and distributed compensation to eligible claimants following established criteria. Aggregate statistics were published while individual claimant identities and specific payout amounts remain confidential.
