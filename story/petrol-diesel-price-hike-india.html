Petrol & Diesel Prices Surge in India: Nayara and Shell Lead Sharp Hike

India is witnessing a major fuel price shock, led by private players Nayara Energy and Shell—marking one of the sharpest increases in recent times.

⛽ What Changed (Key Numbers)

🔺 Nayara Energy

  • Petrol: ↑ ~₹5 per litre
  • Diesel: ↑ ~₹3 per litre

🔺 Shell India

  • Petrol: ↑ ₹7.4 per litre
  • Diesel: ↑ ₹25 per litre
👉 Diesel saw one of the sharpest increases—₹25 per litre

In cities like Bengaluru, petrol prices have reached around ₹119.85, while diesel has moved into the ₹123–₹133 range.

🌍 Why This Sudden Price Hike

  • Global crude oil prices crossed $100 per barrel
  • Iran conflict and Middle East tensions disrupted supply
  • Risk to Strait of Hormuz affecting global oil flow

🏭 Why Private Players Increased Prices First

  • No government subsidy support
  • No pricing restrictions
  • Fully market-linked pricing
👉 Private companies react immediately to global oil changes

📉 Current Fuel Market Situation

Private Pumps PSU Pumps
Prices rising quickly Prices mostly stable
Market-driven Government influenced

⚠️ Impact on Consumers

  • Higher transport costs
  • Increase in goods prices (inflation risk)
  • Logistics and supply chain cost increase
👉 Diesel price hike has a bigger impact than petrol

🔮 What Happens Next

Scenario 1: Oil prices stay high

  • PSU companies may increase prices
  • Nationwide fuel hike possible

Scenario 2: Government intervention

  • Temporary price stability

🧾 Final Insight

This is not just a fuel price hike—it signals rising inflation pressure and potential changes in India’s fuel pricing strategy.

👉 Global events are directly impacting Indian fuel prices and economy
