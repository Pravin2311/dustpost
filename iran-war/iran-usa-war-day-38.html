🔥 Lebanon: The Ceasefire's Fatal Flaw

Why this matters:

  • US/Israel claim Lebanon is outside ceasefire scope
  • Iran insists regional conflicts are interconnected
  • Israeli airstrikes now hit Christian suburbs east of Beirut
  • Hezbollah rocket attacks target northern Israel daily
  • UN reports 1.1+ million displaced in Lebanon alone

This geographic loophole could trigger full ceasefire collapse within hours.
