⚠️ Day 37: Ceasefire Exists — But Is Breaking Down

The 14-day ceasefire that began on April 8 is already under severe stress:

  • Both sides disagree on scope: US/Israel exclude Lebanon; Iran demands inclusion
  • Iran claims violations have occurred via Israeli strikes
  • White House press secretary Karoline Leavitt defended Trump’s “whole civilization” threat as “a very strong threat that led to results”
  • Trump warns: “Stronger military action” if deal fails

*Reality: This is a “pause under tension”—not peace.

🔥 Lebanon: The Ceasefire’s Blind Spot

Why it matters:

  • US and Israel claim Lebanon is outside ceasefire scope
  • Iran insists regional conflicts are interconnected
  • Hezbollah launched rocket barrages into northern Israel in retaliation
  • UN reports 1.1 million displaced in Lebanon alone

This geographic loophole could unravel the entire truce.

⚛️ Nuclear Issue: The Core Stalemate

As talks begin, the biggest obstacle remains unresolved:

  • US Demand: Permanent halt to uranium enrichment; no nuclear weapons
  • Iran Position: “Right to peaceful nuclear energy is non-negotiable”
  • Context: No mention of nuclear program in initial ceasefire terms

Without compromise here, long-term peace is impossible.
